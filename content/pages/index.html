Questions

Before you book a call.

More detail on moving away from a reseller is on the migration page.

Is ERPNext really free?

The software is open source, so there is no licence fee and no charge per user. You pay for hosting, and for people who know the system well enough to set it up and keep it healthy.

Where is our system hosted?

On Frappe Cloud, run by the company that makes ERPNext, or on a server of your choosing. The account is in your company's name either way.

How do you charge?

A fixed price for a fixed scope, agreed before work starts. Ongoing support is a flat monthly fee. Nothing is priced per user.

We already run ERPNext through another company. Can you take over?

Yes. If you are on a white-label reseller plan, we move you onto your own site. If you already own your site, we start with an audit and take over support from there.

What size of company do you work with?

ERPNext suits trading, distribution, manufacturing and services companies best. Our work is aimed at teams of roughly ten to two hundred people using the system.
