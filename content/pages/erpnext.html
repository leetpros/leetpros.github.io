Five steps. Nothing goes live until your numbers match.

Your reseller keeps running the whole time. You compare the copy against the live system, and only then do we switch.

  1. A 15-minute fit call

    We ask what you pay, how many people use the system and which modules you rely on. If moving won't save you real money, we say so on the call.

Questions

What you'll want to know first.

Something else on your mind? Write to hello@leetpros.com.

Will my team have to learn a new system?

No. It is the same ERPNext with your data, your reports and your print formats. Most people notice a new web address and nothing else.

How long does the move take?

Typically two to four weeks from the day we receive your backup, most of it spent on your checks of the staging copy. The switch itself is done over a weekend.

What if my reseller won't hand over a backup?

It is your data, and you are entitled to ask for it in writing; we give you the wording. If only a partial backup is available, we tell you what can and can't be rebuilt before you commit to the move.

We use the reseller's own HR or payroll app. Is that a problem?

It can be. Some add-ons can't be installed outside the reseller's servers. The audit finds them, and we replace each with standard ERPNext features or a small custom app before the cut-over, so payroll runs on the new site from day one.

Where will the new site be hosted?

On Frappe Cloud, run by the company that makes ERPNext, or on a server of your choosing. Either way the account is in your company's name and you receive the hosting invoice directly.

What happens to our tax setup, FBR invoicing and print formats?

They are on the audit list. Each is tested on the staging copy with a real invoice before we cut over, and you approve the result.

Do we have to take the care plan?

No. Once the site is set up you can run it yourselves. The plan is there if you'd like updates, fixes and small changes handled for a flat monthly fee.

We aren't on ERPNext yet. Can you set it up from scratch?

Yes. We implement ERPNext for companies moving off spreadsheets or older accounting software, on a site you own from the first day. See the full list of services.
